Research directions.

Behavioral experiments and quantitative models reveal the mechanisms underlying visual cognition and decision-making.

01 / FACE PERCEPTION

Faces, identity & familiarity

How facial identity is represented and recognized, and how variability and similarity shape unfamiliar face matching.

02 / METACOGNITION

Confidence & uncertainty

Information-theoretic approaches to confidence, metacognitive efficiency, and the evaluation of perceptual decisions.

03 / ATTENTION

Attention & cognitive control

Selective attention, feature integration, Garner interference, and the architecture of cognitive processing.

04 / NUMBER COGNITION

Numbers & integration

How people perceive, compare, and integrate symbolic numerical information using competing decision strategies.
